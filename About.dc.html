01 — Who I am

I'm based in Kendall Park, New Jersey, and I care about engineering that solves real problems for people who are usually an afterthought in product design. That's what led me to found VividSense — an org building low-cost assistive hardware, from prosthetics to navigation wearables.

Outside of VividSense, I split my time between competitive robotics (VEX), rocketry (American Rocketry Challenge), and LaunchX, a youth entrepreneurship program where I now sit on the executive board helping other student teams get their products built.
