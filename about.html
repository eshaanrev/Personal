About

Engineering should earn its complexity.

I care about work that is technically serious and still understandable, affordable, and useful to the person holding it.

I am a student at South Brunswick High School in New Jersey. I founded VividSense to build assistive technology for people who are often priced out or treated as an afterthought in product design.

My work moves between embedded systems, product development, competitive robotics, aerospace, and web software. I like projects where a physical constraint forces better thinking.

Outside my own builds, I help other students develop products through XSTEM, teach robotics and front-end development, and mentor younger makers.
